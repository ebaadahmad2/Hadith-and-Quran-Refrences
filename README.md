# Topic 1: Character of the Prophet ﷺ
# Qur'an (68:4): "You are of a great moral character."
# Lesson: Allah literally praised the Prophet ﷺ himself — his whole vibe was kindness, patience and respect. We should try act like him fr.

# Hadith 1 (Imam Ahmad): "I was only sent to perfect good character."
# Lesson: The whole point of Islam is good manners. If you're rude and harsh, you're missing the point.

# Hadith 2 (Bukhari): "His character was the Quran." — Aisha (RA)
# Lesson: The Prophet ﷺ didn't just recite the Quran, he lived it. We should too not just read it and close it.

# Hadith 3 (Tirmidhi): "Nothing will be heavier on the Day of Judgement than good manners."
# Lesson: Good character counts more than you think on the Day of Judgement — it's actually a big deal.

# ✅ Implementation: I started greeting people first with salaam and stopped being rude back when someone annoyed me. It's harder than it sounds ngl but I kept it up.

# Topic 2: Story of Lut (AS)
# Qur'an (54:33–35): "We sent a storm of stones upon the people of Lut… except his family, whom We saved."
# Lesson: Allah doesn't play — those who kept rejecting the truth got punished, but the believers were always protected.

# Hadith 1: The angels reassured Ibrahim (AS) that Lut (AS) would be saved.
# Lesson: Allah never leaves His believers hanging, no matter how bad things get around them.

# Hadith 2: Lut (AS) called his people for years and barely anyone listened — even his own wife turned against him.
# Lesson: Doing the right thing can feel lonely but you stay on it anyway. Even a prophet got betrayed by his own wife.

# Hadith 3 (Tirmidhi): "Allah cursed whoever does the action of the people of Lut."
# Lesson: Some sins are no joke — don't normalise what Allah has forbidden no matter what society says.

# ✅ Implementation: In a group chat people were saying some dodgy stuff and instead of joining in I just didn't reply. Small thing but that's basically what Lut (AS) was about — not going along with what's wrong.

# Topic 3: Khushoo' in Salah
# Qur'an (23:1–2): "Successful are the believers — those who are humble in their prayers."
# Lesson: Khushoo' (focus in salah) is literally the first sign of a successful believer. It's not extra credit, it's the baseline.

# Hadith 1 (Abu Dawud): "A person may pray and only one tenth gets recorded for them."
# Lesson: If you're just rushing through salah thinking about other stuff, you're barely getting any reward. Quality over quantity.

# Hadith 2 (Muslim): "The first thing to be taken from this Ummah will be khushoo'."
# Lesson: In this age with phones and constant distractions, khushoo' is already disappearing — we need to protect it.

# Hadith 3: "Khushoo' is only for the one who empties their heart completely for the prayer."
# Lesson: You have to actually prepare before salah — put the phone down and get your head right before you start.

# ✅ Implementation: For Maghrib I put my phone in another room and made wudu properly without rushing. I actually thought about what Al-Fatiha means while I was saying it. It genuinely hit different — #felt way more calm after. Gonna try do this every day.
